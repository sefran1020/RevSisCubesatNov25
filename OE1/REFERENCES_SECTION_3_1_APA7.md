## Formatting Notes for APA 7th Edition

1. **DOI format:** Use https://doi.org/ prefix (not doi: or dx.doi.org)
2. **Journal volume/issue:** Volume in italics, issue in parentheses (not italicized)
3. **Article numbers:** Use "Article" before number for journals using article numbers instead of page ranges
4. **Conference proceedings:** Include full conference name, page range, publisher, and DOI
5. **Multiple authors:** Use "&" before last author (not "and")
6. **Titles:** Only capitalize first word, first word after colon, and proper nouns
7. **Journal titles:** Capitalize all major words, use italics
8. **Access dates:** Not required for DOI-assigned content
